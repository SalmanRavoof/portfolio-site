How I work

I ask questions before I write

Send a title, an outline, or just a rough goal. Before anyone writes a word, you get back questions about who this is for and what it needs to do.

I do the research myself

I test the tools and run the code. If a piece makes a claim, I read the source it came from, and I can show you that source later.

I deliver a draft you can publish

It arrives on the agreed date, formatted for your CMS, with internal links and meta copy suggested. I co-founded a design agency before this, so diagrams and screenshots come with it when they help.

I revise until it's right

Reasonable revisions are part of the price. Most drafts need one light pass, and if something misses the brief, I fix it.

Questions clients ask

What do you write?

Technical tutorials, platform comparisons, migration guides, and the evaluations executives read before signing something expensive. If it sits between developer documentation and a boardroom memo, I've probably written it.

How fast can you turn a piece around?

One to two weeks from brief to draft, depending on how much research it needs. Tell me your deadline in the first email and I'll tell you honestly whether I can hit it.

Do you ghostwrite?

Yes, and it costs a bit more, since I'm giving up the byline. The ghostwritten pieces here are shown with permission. Your name, my drafts, full confidentiality if you want it.

What does it cost?

Depends on how you prefer to buy. Per project, per word, discounted by volume, or a part-time retainer if you need steady output. Send the brief and I'll quote against whichever model you already use.

What if you're not the right fit?

I'll say so in the first reply and point you to someone better suited. A mismatch wastes your time and gets me nothing worth showing.
