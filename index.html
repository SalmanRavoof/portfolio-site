Questions clients ask

What do you write?

Technical tutorials, product-led content, platform comparisons, migration guides, original research, and the executive-facing evaluations that precede big platform decisions. If it sits between "developer documentation" and "boardroom memo," it's my lane.

How fast can you turn a piece around?

Depends on depth and research load. A standard long-form piece typically takes one to two weeks from brief to draft. Tell me your deadline in the first email and I'll tell you honestly whether it's doable.

Do you ghostwrite?

Yes. Five of the pieces in my portfolio are ghostwritten for Multidots and shown with the client's permission. Your byline, my drafts, full confidentiality if you need it.

What does it cost?

Per-project pricing based on scope, research depth, and rights. Send me the brief and I'll quote a flat number, no hourly meters running.

What if you're not the right fit?

I'll say so in the first reply and point you to someone who is. A mismatch wastes your budget and my portfolio.
